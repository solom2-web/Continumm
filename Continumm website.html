Policies

No-Show & Payment Policy

Continumm books appointments for septic and well contractors under a simple, aligned-incentive model. Here's exactly how billing and no-shows work.

The Paid Per Shown Appointment model

You're charged a per-appointment fee only when a scheduled appointment is attended by the homeowner — meaning the contractor arrives at the property at the agreed time and the homeowner is present.

Card on file

By working with Continumm, you authorize us to store your payment method securely via Stripe and automatically charge the agreed per-appointment fee once a show is confirmed.

No-show handling

If the homeowner doesn't show, you're not charged for that appointment. Instead, a credit is applied toward a future replacement appointment. Credits don't expire while your engagement with Continumm is active.

Disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, let us know within 7 days of the charge so we can review it. Good-faith errors are refunded in full.

Ending the engagement

Either party can end the engagement with 7 days' notice. Any pending credits are honored during that notice period.
